Methodology

Every calculator turns your measurements into an area in square feet, multiplies by depth for a volume, and converts to the units suppliers sell: cubic yards, tons, bags or pallets.

Cubic feet = area (sq ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. A circle's area is π × (diameter ÷ 2)².

Bag counts always round up. The "extra for waste" percentage is added to the area before anything else, to cover uneven ground, settling and cuts.
