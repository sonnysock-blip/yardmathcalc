## Description
Plan your next yard project before you buy a single bag. The Yard Project Planner is a 14-page printable PDF that helps you measure odd-shaped spaces, estimate how much material you need, and compare prices, all in US units.

**What's inside (14 pages, US Letter 8.5 x 11 in):**
- Cover page
- How to measure irregular areas: split any shape into rectangles, triangles and circles, with diagrams and a worked example
- Project overview: goals, budget (estimate vs. actual), and timeline
- Measurement worksheet with a full-page sketch grid
- Material estimate worksheets with formulas and fill-in lines:
  - Mulch, soil and compost
  - Gravel and stone (cubic yards and tons)
  - Concrete (slabs and post holes, 40/60/80 lb bags)
  - Sod and grass seed
- Quick-reference charts: mulch coverage by depth, bags per cubic yard by bag size, approximate material weights, concrete bag yields, sod piece and pallet sizes, and grass seed rates for common species
- Bag vs. bulk cost comparison worksheet
- Supplier quote comparison table with questions to ask
- Seasonal yard checklist: spring, summer, fall and winter
- Notes and sketch page

**Good for:** mulching beds, gravel paths, patios and pads, new lawns, raised beds, and anyone who wants to stop making a second trip to the garden center.

**Designed to print:** clean layout with light ink use. Print at home or at a print shop, as many copies as you need for your own personal use.

**Pairs with the free calculators at yardmathcalc.com** if you'd like to check your numbers online.

Chart figures are rounded planning estimates. Always follow your product labels and your supplier's numbers.

**Digital download:** this is an instant-download PDF. Nothing physical will be shipped.
