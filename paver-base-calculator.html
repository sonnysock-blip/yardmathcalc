How it's calculated

The calculator works out three quantities from your area, which already includes the extra percentage.

Base (cu yd) = area (sq ft) x base depth (in) / 12 / 27 / (1 - compaction %)
Base (tons) = cubic yards x 1.4
Bedding sand (cu ft) = area x sand depth (in) / 12
Pavers = area x 144 / (paver length x width in inches)

Example: a 10 x 20 ft patio is 200 sq ft, or 210 sq ft with 5% extra. A 4 in compacted base needs 210 x 4 / 12 / 27 = 2.59 cubic yards in place. Crushed stone shrinks about 15% when compacted, so order 2.59 / 0.85 = 3.05 cubic yards, about 4.3 tons. One inch of sand is 17.5 cu ft, or 35 half-cubic-foot bags. With 4 x 8 in pavers (4.5 per sq ft) you need 945 pavers.

Crushed stone base weighs about 1.35 to 1.45 tons per cubic yard depending on gradation and moisture. Use a dense-graded 3/4 in minus base (with fines) so it locks together, and compact it in 2 to 3 inch lifts.

Common questions

How deep should a paver base be?

About 4 inches of compacted gravel for walkways and 4 to 6 inches for patios. Use 6 to 8 inches in freezing climates or clay soil, and 8 to 12 inches under driveways.

How much sand goes under pavers?

About 1 inch of coarse concrete sand, screeded flat over the compacted base. Sand thicker than about 1.5 inches tends to shift and cause uneven pavers.

How many 4 x 8 pavers are in a square foot?

4.5. Each 4 x 8 in paver covers 32 square inches, and a square foot is 144 square inches.

Do I need to compact the gravel base?

Yes. Compact it in lifts of 2 to 3 inches with a plate compactor. An uncompacted base settles and lets pavers sink and rock.
